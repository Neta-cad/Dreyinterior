      Questions people ask

      Do I need to measure my space?

      No. We visit your home or office and take every measurement ourselves, so each piece fits exactly.

      Do you visit and deliver outside Lagos?

      Yes. We visit and deliver in every state in Nigeria. Choose your state when you book.

      How does payment work?

      You pay a site visit fee when you book. If you go ahead with an order, that fee is deducted from the final price. After you approve your quote, you pay a deposit to start building, and the balance is paid on delivery.

      Can you build something that is not on the website?

      Yes. Everything is built from scratch, so if you can describe it or show us a picture, we can build it.

      Have a space in mind?

      Book a site visit and we will come to you, measure, and plan your furniture together.
